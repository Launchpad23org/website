What do we teach?

Weekly Topics

Every launch, we offer multiple topics for our cadets to choose from, based upon their interests. You will find a running accounting below. Note that some topics are taught as a series, and many respond to individual student requests and interests.

Missions

Every semester, our cadets choose a Mission (project) aligned with their goals designed to challenge them to practice and demonstrate high agency. The cadets present their results to an audience of family and friends at Demo Day, held at the end of each semester.

Applied Skills

We use challenges and strategy games to teach everything from critical thinking to teamwork and communication skills. We develop all of our challenges in house, and have an ever-growing library of challenges and puzzles in our Challenge Codex and Puzzle Warehouse designed to challenge even the brightest minds. We are working to make the Challenge Codex publicly available.
