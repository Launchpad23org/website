Privacy Policy and Disclaimers

Thank you for visiting the Launchpad23 website and reviewing our Privacy Policy. Launchpad23 is committed to protecting your privacy and securing the personal information made available to us when you access our websites, applications, and other online platforms. This Privacy Policy describes what information is made available to Launchpad23, what information Launchpad23 collects, how that information is used, and how information is stored when you visit Launchpad23 websites or accounts on third-party websites and applications.

Information Collected and Stored Automatically

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. When you visit, GitHub logs your Internet Protocol (IP) address and stores it for security purposes, whether or not you have a GitHub account. Launchpad23 does not receive or use these logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and GitHub's note on GitHub Pages data collection.

Some features of this website are delivered by Google, LLC:

  • The website's typeface is loaded from Google Fonts;
  • The Examples page reads its list of resources and topics from a Google Sheet; and
  • Registration and other forms are hosted on Google Forms.

When your browser loads these features, Google receives the standard technical information needed to deliver them, such as your IP address and browser type, under the Google Privacy Policy.

Launchpad23 does not use analytics or advertising tools on this website, and does not use this information to identify individual visitors.

Commercial Marketing

Launchpad23 does not collect or use information for commercial marketing. Launchpad23 will send general updates to registered families about activities, events, registrations, new programs, and similar opportunities, but families may opt out of such general updates by contacting team@launchpad23.org.

Personal Information that You Voluntarily Provide

You are not required to provide any personal information to us to access information on our websites. If you choose to provide us with personal information, such as by sending a message to an email address on this website or by filling out and submitting a form through our website, we will use that information to respond to your message or to fulfil the stated purpose of the communication.

Sharing of Personal Information You Voluntarily Provide Directly to Launchpad23

Launchpad23 will not share information you voluntarily provide with other entities, except with such third-party contractors and applications as described in the next paragraph, and then only to the extent required to respond to your message and/or process your request.

Information Automatically Collected by Third-Party Websites or Applications that facilitate Launchpad23 business processes (Service Providers)

Launchpad23 maintains accounts with, and facilitates business functions through, third-party service providers of websites and applications. These third-party website and application service providers may themselves automatically collect and store additional information about you, in accordance with their terms of service and privacy policies. Launchpad23 does not control what these third-party service providers do with the information they collect.

Personal Information Voluntarily Provided to Launchpad23 on Third-Party Websites or Applications

Launchpad23 maintains accounts on third-party websites and applications, such as social media platforms. When interacting with the public on third-party websites and applications, Launchpad23 may request that you voluntarily provide information to Launchpad23 (for example, Launchpad23 may request your information to register for an event hosted by Launchpad23). These third-party website and application service providers may also collect the information you voluntarily provide, in accordance with their terms of service and privacy policies. Launchpad23 does not control what these third-party service providers do with the information they collect.

Use Caution When Voluntarily Providing Sensitive Information

Remember that internet communications are not necessarily secure from interception. If your communication is sensitive or includes personal information, you may prefer to send it to Launchpad23 by postal mail or other commercial carriers instead.

Children and Privacy

We believe in the importance of protecting the privacy of children online and do not knowingly contact or collect personal information from children under 13 without prior approval of the child's parent or guardian. Unless otherwise stated, our websites are not intended to solicit information of any kind from children under 13 without prior approval of the child's parent or guardian.

The Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) is a U.S. federal law that applies to the online collection of personal information from children under 13 years of age.

At Launchpad23, we go beyond the protections of COPPA by requiring that a parent or guardian enroll and set up Launchpad23 accounts for any child 17 years of age and under. To verify that a parent or guardian has granted consent, they will need to provide a valid credit card number during the enrollment process. Requiring credit card payment is a commonly used and accepted parental control method, so that parents have the chance to see the payment on their credit card statement, and to alert Launchpad23, if consent was improperly provided.

If consent was improperly provided, parents can contact team@launchpad23.org and request assistance closing their child's account, if created, and permanent deletion of any associated data collected about their child.

Once a child's account has been created, parents can request additional information about privacy, or assistance, from team@launchpad23.org.
