Families Affected by Bullying

No child should ever experience bullying.

We believe what you know first hand: no child should ever experience bullying, and unchecked bullying is an unacceptable failure of the many systems that are supposed to protect children at school.

We also know that your child is unique, as was their experience with bullying. Their path back to trusting peers, and trusting group environments will also be unique, which is where we can help.

At Launchpad23, safety is our top priority, and that absolutely includes creating an environment that is emotionally safe for each and every child.

We work hard to build a culture where the peer norms are to encourage and support, and our students quickly become friends, no matter how many miles separate them, because they know they can count on each other for support.

With that said, we know that it is hard for many children that have experienced bullying to trust a new environment, which is why we suggest that you contact us before enrolling in the program.

We will meet with you, and your child if they are ready, to discuss what we can do to make joining Launchpad23 as comfortable as possible.

We look forward to helping your child start a new chapter, and to reminding your child just how wonderful and amazing other children can be in the right environment.
