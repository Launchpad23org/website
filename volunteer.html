Volunteer

Your strengths are your tools and our programs your canvas.

We have multiple ways to contribute, with time commitments designed to fit your life. Are you ready to create?

Existing volunteers: sign-up to host sessions here!

Professionals

Speak. The perfect option for busy individuals that wish to share their passion and knowledge without an ongoing commitment.

Teach. We'll give you the support and tools to inspire the next generation, whether you want to teach weekly, monthly, quarterly, or once per year.

Build. Leverage your expertise to help our headquarters functions (e.g., technology, accounting, legal, HR, grant writing, and marketing).

In all cases, we flex to your availability, so volunteer with the confidence that this will absolutely work with your schedule.

Student Internships

If you are ready to break out of academia, we look forward to giving you the opportunity to build and learn side-by-side with the leaders of the company.

You will:

  • Work one-on-one with leadership;
  • Lead a project aligned to your goals; and
  • Receive specific feedback, letters of recommendation, and support.
