The Riddle

Within each picture hides a name, A city known to the world for its fame. A famous landmark marks the place, Though you will not see it in this space. Look past the scene the AI drew, The subtle guides will point you true. Solve every city that you find, As another puzzle runs behind. Numbers paired will light the way, Through a square where letters stay. When all are gathered, line by line, The final answer you’ll divine.

The Catch: You may only guess once per clue / image, so be sure!
