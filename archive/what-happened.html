What Happened?

"History will be kind to me for I intend to write it."

Winston Churchill

Today's challenge is simple (in concept, not in fulfillment):

  • Dates. Each number below corresponds to a date.
  • Events. Figure out the most consequential event that happened that year.
  • Cities. Find the city where the event happened.
  • Letters. Use the first letter of the city to decipher the word.
  • Answer. The answer is the word AND a sentence explaining the significance of the word.

You will be ranked based upon how many challenges you correctly answer:

3 - 4

5 - 6

7+

The Challenges

1) You may compete as an individual, or on a team, but not both.

2) You may solve the challenges in any order.

3) No cheating.

4) Ask if you have questions! You may ask for three hints. Use them wisely . . .

5) You must submit all of your guesses at one time to qualify for Galaxy Cup points. The team with the highest number of correct answers will earn 20 Galaxy Cup points for their launch.
