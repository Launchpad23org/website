Project BRIGHT

Access for curious children, irrespective of their ability to pay.

At Launchpad23, Inc., we are dedicated to creating access for curious children, irrespective of their ability to pay, through Project BRIGHT (Bringing Resources and Involvement to Gifted Homes Together).

If you know a curious child that would benefit from participation in Launchpad23, but whose family does not have the ability to pay for the program, we encourage you to contact us.

A few things to consider:

  • Our sessions are open to children ages 7 to 17 years old, and we welcome children from public schools and homeschools alike;
  • All children must have level B2 (or above) English language skills;
  • All children must have parental consent to participate;
  • All children must want to participate; and
  • All children need a laptop or desktop computer, and a stable internet connection to participate.

Please do not provide any personally identifiable information about the child when you reach out, for privacy reasons.

Instead, give us a general overview of why you think the child would be a good fit for the program, in your opinion. We will work with you to involve the child's parents, and complete the next steps in the process.

If you have questions about the program, or need additional information, feel free to use the same link to send us your questions. We're always happy to hear from people trying to help!
