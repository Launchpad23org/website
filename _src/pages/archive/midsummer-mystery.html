A Midsummer Mystery

Transport yourself to a world of intrigue and challenge in the L23 Midsummer Mystery.

Preserved from the old site. On Wix, each stage was its own page and players clicked a symbol to advance. Answers were not on the pages.

Stage 0

The Quest Begins

Ahh! So you think yourself brave enough to venture into this midsummer mystery?

Welcome then, indeed, and good luck.

Be forewarned, though, because only one path leads to your quest's goal, and all others lead to peril.

To keep you safe, and aid you on your journey, you have a band of loyal companions venturing forth with you today. You will need every one of them to emerge out of this quest with the answers you seek.

Now, for the question, the only question that matters in this realm until it is solved, as the future of everything depends upon it:

Where is The Scepter of Amanar?

There is a reason you have not heard of The Scepter before: Rumor has it that The Scepter bestows power upon the person that possesses it, but the truth is far worse.

The scepter also turns anyone that uses it into a being of pure evil.

The last time someone wielded The Scepter, it brought war to all of the Hidden Kingdoms for over one thousand years.

The Scepter must be found and returned to its resting place in Mount Grafru before war once again consumes the Hidden Kingdoms.

The High Council sent many parties such as yours to try and find The Scepter. The heroes that return The Scepter will be rewarded, but success matters more than the reward.

Each challenge will give you a clue to help you find The Scepter, but beware of the trickery and deceit of the thief.

When you are ready, click the symbol below to arrive at the first stop in your journey.

Stage 1

Mount Grafru: The Dragons

Your journey starts at Mount Grafru, where you must traverse the cavern that once held The Scepter in the hope that you can follow the trail of the thief from there.

The trick is the dragons that live in the cavern, whose job it was to protect The Scepter.

They have been driven somewhat mad by the loss of The Scepter, and nobody has been able to enter the cavern since the theft.

The wizards of the High Council have pored over the ancient scrolls in the hope of finding a clue to help you calm the dragons and allow for your passage.

Solve the riddle, quiet the dragons, and your journey can begin.

To calm their might and still their flame,

Three voices must sing in gentle acclaim.

What secret tune must you employ,

To soothe the beast and bring them joy?

Ask a mother and she will not lie,

The answer is . . .

Stage 2

The Door

Ah! A secret door! Yet, you must find a way past. Hear the riddle, draw the solution, call for aide, and steps forward will be made.

I guard the realm with silent might,

My lofty walls reach day and night.

Within my halls, both grand and vast,

Echoes of the ages past.

My spires pierce the heavens' veil,

Legends in my stones prevail.

What am I, with secrets deep,

A stronghold where the ancients sleep?

Stage 3

Krivath: The Chess Board

You track news of the thief to Krivath, but when you arrive, your party begins to notice that you do not feel welcome.

Nobody will speak to you, save a young man sitting at a chess board, waiting for his partner to return.

He claims to have the clue you need, but only if you can tell him how to checkmate his friend in three moves. The answer is your key.

Stage 4

The Thief's Riddle

The thief has no fear of your pursuit, as he does not think you observant enough to catch him. To prove his point, he left you a riddle, and is waiting to see if you can perceive the obvious answer. If you do, you will find where he hid the scepter.

From the thief:

"In a realm where the loquacious congregate,

A sagacious mind must navigate.

To discern the veracity of what’s posited,

One must not be easily mollified.

Among the erudite, a clandestine lore,

Where fulminations obscure the core.

A garrulous prevaricator spins a tale,

Ensuring credulous minds to fail.

In this intricate labyrinth of lexicon,

Decipher the riddle with acumen strong.

I am a nexus of verbiage and guile,

In a discourse where truth is exiled.

What am I, that requires such acuity,

To perceive through my verbosity and obliquity?"

Stage 5

The Fog of Deception (Maze)

Alas, your journey is not done, and the thief it turns out is not just a thief, but a powerful wizard that is none too happy about losing the prize he spent his lifetime trying to steal.

He casts a magical spell across the lands, and a Fog of Deception has rolled in, turning roads and streets into mazes of dead ends and false promises.

Find your way and find the answer that unlocks the next stretch of your journey.

Stage 6

The Mage's Riddle

You come across a mage that claims to have the code needed to open the magical iron gates of Mount Grafru.

He asks that you prove you are wise by solving his riddle:

Start with the launch date of the first human mission to land on the moon.

Add to the year the result of this equation: The apogee distance from Earth to the Moon divided by the equatorial radius of the Earth, rounded to the nearest tenth.

Add to the year the distance from Sol to Jupiter in astronomical units, rounded to the nearest tenth.

Subtract from the year the average gravitational pull of Earth in meters per second squared.

Subtract from the year the number of rocky planets in the solar system.

Subtract from the day the number of the Apollo mission that first landed on the moon.

Subtract from the day the number of Mercury astronauts.

Subtract from the day the number of human-created satellites to enter the heliopause.

Check your answer before you knock on the door, or it shall not open to you, ever.

Stage 7

Heroes!

You did it! Your merry band has tracked the thief, recovered the scepter, and returned it to its proper resting place. The dragons are calm. The realm is calm, but you have reason to celebrate:

20 Galaxy Cup points for your launch!

Ten more GCPs if you can answer the final riddle:

In hindsight, what was the optimal approach to the mystery?
