We ♥ Homeschoolers

We love homeschoolers, because many of us are homeschoolers.

At Launchpad23, opportunities abound for homeschoolers, because we are building this program to give our own children the very best educational experience possible.

  • Weekly online classes that offer your child a choice of topics to learn about, life skills, and a global community to share their interests.
  • Weekly Challenge Hour, free to all homeschoolers, to bring our community together across the Nation and around the globe.
  • A moderated, online community where your child can meet fellow peers, and you can meet other parents on this journey.
  • Co-op discounts and pay-what-you-can programs to make the experience accessible to as many homeschoolers as possible.

If this sounds like exactly what you have been looking for, we look forward to seeing your child at our next launch!

  • Our launches (sessions) occur every Wednesday for 90 minutes each, with times convenient for all U.S. homeschoolers, and many international homeschoolers (even Australia for 5 months!).
  • We offer a 14-day free trial, with no charge if the program is not the right fit for your child.
  • After the free trial, the cost is US$85 per month for the first child, and US$20 per sibling, and again, we have a pay-what-you-can program for families in need of assistance.
  • If your child has specialized needs that you would like to discuss, please contact us, as we want to help.
