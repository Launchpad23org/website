At Launchpad23, we are on a mission to get humanity firing on all cylinders.

Launchpad23 is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization dedicated to providing an online space where children can:

  • Find peers from around the world with similar interests; and
  • Grow the skills each child needs to make a difference.

Foundational to our approach is the knowledge that your child is unique, and therefore your child needs and deserves a learning journey tailored to their strengths and opportunities for growth.

We also forgo teachers chained to one-size-fits-all curriculum in favor of subject matter experts passionately teaching topics they love, using our hands-on approach that challenges each child to dynamically synthesize and apply their new knowledge.

We welcome the opportunity to answer any questions you may have, and look forward to welcoming your child to a future launch!
